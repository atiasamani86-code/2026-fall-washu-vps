The Forbidden Zone

My Reflection

What I found interesting about The Forbidden Zone is the idea that collecting things can be a creative process. Before reading this, I mostly thought of websites as places to share information or show finished work. But this article made me think about how a website itself can become a form of art.

I also liked the idea that we don't always need to know exactly what we are looking for. Sometimes, finding unexpected connections between images, ideas, and memories can lead to something new.

This connects to my own experience with Are.na because I started by collecting things I liked, but slowly I began to see connections between them. It made me realize that organizing information can also be a way of expressing who we are.
