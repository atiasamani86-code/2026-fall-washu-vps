A Personal Ritual: Cooking

Cooking is one of the most important rituals in my life. It is not just about preparing food. It helps me slow down, relax, and take a break from my busy schedule.

I enjoy choosing ingredients, preparing them, and watching them become a meal. When I cook, I focus on the present moment instead of worrying about everything I need to do.

Cooking also connects me to my memories and culture. Making Iranian food reminds me of home, family, and the people I love.

For me, cooking is a combination of creativity, care, and attention. It reminds me that happiness does not always come from big achievements. Sometimes, it comes from simple everyday moments.
