The Archive

One human made creation that brings out the best in me is the archive. I have always been interested in history, art, and human stories. For me, an archive is not just a place to keep old documents or photographs. It is a way to connect the past with the present.

When I work with archival materials, I become more curious and patient. I start asking questions about whose stories were saved, whose voices were forgotten, and why.

I think archives bring out the best in me because they help me think more deeply and understand different human experiences.

Unlike technology that helps us work faster, an archive encourages me to slow down, observe, and question what I see.

For me, that is more valuable than simply being productive.
