Reading Response 1: Why Restaurant Websites Are Good

The article made me think about a restaurant website as more than a place to find the menu, hours, and address. A good website can communicate the personality and atmosphere of the restaurant before a customer arrives. I liked the example of Astro Restaurant because its imperfect and inconsistent design made it feel personal rather than unprofessional.

I was also interested in the tension between convenience and creative freedom. Platforms such as Squarespace, Facebook, and Instagram make it easier for restaurant workers to update information, but templates can also make many websites look the same. The example of Dimes shows that the tool is not the entire problem. A modern platform can still produce a unique website when the people designing it make expressive choices.

This reading connects to my restaurant menu project for Pars Persian Market. Its website could do more than list Persian foods and prices. It could share the stories, ingredients, traditions, and people connected to the food. I especially liked the idea of restaurants acting as publishers, or even as “physical blogs,” because a website can preserve the changing life and culture of a place.
