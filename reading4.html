Do Artifacts Have Ethics?

Reading Response — L. M. Sacasas (2014)

What I found most interesting about this reading is the idea that technology is never completely neutral. Sacasas argues that the ethical questions surrounding technology are not only about how we use it, but also about how it changes our habits, behaviors, and relationships.

One question that stayed with me was how technology changes our experience of time. I started thinking about the Pomodoro Technique, which I have been researching for our timekeeping project. A simple timer does more than count minutes. By dividing work into periods of focus and rest, it can influence how we organize our attention and experience productivity.

I also found the questions about what technology encourages us to notice or ignore particularly important. For example, a smartphone can help us stay organized, but its notifications can constantly interrupt our attention.

As a designer, this reading made me think about responsibility beyond functionality. A product might work perfectly and still encourage habits we do not want. I think good design should consider not only what technology allows us to do, but also how it shapes the way we live.
