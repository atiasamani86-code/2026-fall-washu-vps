Eno

My Media Reflection

What I found interesting about Eno was that it was not a traditional documentary. I liked how technology was used to change the way the story was presented. The film showed me that a documentary does not always have to follow the same structure.

I also found it interesting that the film can be different each time someone watches it. This made me think about how technology can become part of the creative process, instead of just being a tool.

As a design student, this made me think about how the system we create can influence the final result. I liked the idea that design is not always about controlling every detail, but sometimes about creating possibilities for something unexpected.
