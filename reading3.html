Reading Response 3: My Website Is a Shifting House Next to a River of Knowledge

I liked the idea that a website does not need to be a perfect or complete archive. Before reading this essay, I mostly thought of a website as a place to present finished work. The essay helped me see it as a living space that can change as I learn and change. I also connected with the idea that having a personal website gives an individual more control than posting only on social media platforms.

My website could be a garden. Right now, I am planting small ideas through my class exercises, reading responses, and projects. Some parts may grow quickly, while others may take more time. Like a garden, the website does not have to be completely finished. I can continue adding to it, changing it, and learning from it over time.

When I make my own website, I can control its structure, colors, typography, and personality. I can experiment, make mistakes, and change things whenever I want. The process also teaches me how the website works. If someone else makes it for me, the result might look polished, but it may not express my ideas and identity in the same personal way.
